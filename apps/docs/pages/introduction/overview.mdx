---
title: Overview
---

Mad Chatter is an offline voice-based AI assistant. It combines large language models with knowledge retrieval, text-to-speech synthesis, speech-to-text input, and interactive video responses into a single local system.

Unlike traditional chatbot systems that rely on cloud services, Mad Chatter operates completely offline. All models and data are stored and executed locally, ensuring privacy, reliability, and availability without requiring an internet connection.

For any questions reach out to any of the contributors of the main repository at https://github.com/sleeepyskies/madchatter.
